*Be careful how you treat an opponent across the board. They might be a god in disguise.*

# Overview of Play
1. Over several turns, players, as gods, will place tiles to create a world, claim nodes on the portfolio board, and complete myths to build up the world and claim victory points. Myths are the links that connect the world tiles and the portfolio board.
2. There are two areas of play. The first is the portfolio board where players claim nodes to active myth actions. The second is the world space where players will place terrain tiles.
3. Goal. The game ends once the tile deck is empty. The victor is the player with the most victory points. Typically, this is the player who completed the most myths over the course of play. In the event of a tie, determine a winner by a fight to the death among tied players.

# Setup
1. **Portfolio Board.** Each player places a claimant marker on the void node at the center of the portfolio board.
2. **Tile Market.** Shuffle the terrain tiles and arrange them in a deck. Draw six tiles and arrange them next to the tile deck. These six starting tiles are the tile market.
3. **Starting Tile.** Players vote to determine which of the tiles in the market should be the starting tile for their world. Break ties at random and place the chosen tile at the center of the world space.

# Sequence of Play
1. Each round is composed of three phases. The first phase is the series phase where each player in turn order will take two basic actions. The second phase is the upkeep phase where action triggers and other upkeep occurs before the parallel phase. The third phase is the parallel phase where players act simultaneously by special actions and discussion.
2. **Series Phase.** \[explain]
3. **Upkeep Phase.** As the first action of the upkeep phase, place a devotee lot at each terrain tile in the world space with a temple. The devotee must match the suite of the terrain tile. Then, in turn order each player will trigger any myth abilities that reference the upkeep phase and draw a lot from the lot bag. Players hold onto their drawn lot until the end of the parallel phase.
4. **Parallel Phase.** Players discuss their drawn lots and agree on how to resolve them. Additionally, any lot on a tile in the world space with a movement icon may be moved to any other tile so long as all players agree to the movement.
	

# Basic Actions
1. **Place a Terrain Tile.** Take a terrain tile from the tile market and place it adjacent to any tile in the world space. For each terrain edge on the placed tile, place a corresponding devotee lot on the placed tile. Immediately draw a new tile from the tile deck to fill the tile market.
	1. **Terrain Edge Mismatches.** If the edges of a placed tile do not match the edges of its adjacent tiles, the active player loses one victory point for each mismatched edge of the placed tile.
2. **Spend a Terrain Tile.** Take a terrain tile from the tile market and place it in the discard pile. For each terrain edge on the discard tile, place a corresponding devotee on tiles in the world space whose suite matches the devotee. The devotees do not need to all be placed on the same tile. If the world space does not contain tiles of matching suite to any devotees, then those devotees can be placed on any tiles.
3. **Search a Myth Deck.** Remove any number of devotees of a single suite from a single tile. Look at X cards from the top of the myth deck with a matching suite, where X is the number of devotees removed from the tile. The player may take one of those myths and add it to their play area. The player does not need to take any of the myths. All myths that are not chosen are then placed on the bottom of their myth deck in any order.
4. **Embody a Myth.** For any myth in a players play area, perform a single action from that myth. These actions vary from myth to myth, but typically they require the player to hold a specific node on the portfolio board. Additionally, myths might refer to specific lot and/or tile combinations in the world space. If the combination is not present, then a player may not take that specific myth action.
	1. **Epithet Rule.** When myth requires a player to hold a certain node on the portfolio board, if the active player does not control the node, but another player does, then the active player may give the node holder a victory point. This allows the active player to take the myth action as though they controlled the node. The epithet rule applies to individual actions. If they active player wishes to use another player's node for multiple actions, then a victory point must be given each time.
5. **Place Temple.** Remove 6 devotees from a single terrain tile and place a temple lot on that terrain tile in the world space. Multiple temples can be placed on the same tile.
